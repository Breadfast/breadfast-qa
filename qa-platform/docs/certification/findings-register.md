## Certification decisions (temporary)
- **Figma analysis — analyze ALL exported frames (2026-07-16).** During certification, `figma_analysis` intentionally feeds **every** exported frame to the AI reasoning step. **Reason:** maximize **Platform Parity** with the manual AI QA Companion (which reviews all frames) — no frame is silently dropped. **Cost:** large boards (60+ frames) are slow and were the root cause of F6; mitigated for now by the 25-min timeout, not by reducing coverage. **Future enhancement (post-certification):** intelligent frame selection (relevance-rank / de-duplicate frames against the story's AC) and/or **batched analysis** for very large boards, so wall-clock scales without lowering coverage. Revisit only after the certification campaign closes; do not lower the timeout or trim frames before that lands.
